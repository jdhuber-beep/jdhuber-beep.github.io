This is Earth's moon. It has almost no air, so the surface temperature changes a lot between day and night. Its gravity is what pulls the ocean tides.

Data for Earth's Moon

  • Diameter 3,474 km
  • Distance from Earth 384,400 km
  • Orbital period 27.3 Earth days
  • Temperature About 120°C in daylight and -130°C at night
