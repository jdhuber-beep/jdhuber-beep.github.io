Pluto was called the ninth planet for a long time. It is now grouped as a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt. Its largest moon, Charon, is big enough that the two orbit a point between them.

Data for Pluto

  • Diameter 2,376 km
  • Distance from the Sun 5.9 billion km
  • Orbital period 248 Earth years
  • Temperature About -229°C
