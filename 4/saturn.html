Saturn is the planet with the obvious rings. Those rings are mostly chunks of ice. The planet itself is a gas giant, and it is light for its size.

Data for Saturn

  • Diameter 116,460 km
  • Distance from the Sun 1.4 billion km
  • Orbital period 29.5 Earth years
  • Temperature About -140°C at the cloud tops
