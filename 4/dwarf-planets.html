  2. Dwarf Planets

Dwarf planets orbit the Sun and are round enough to be planet-like, but they have not cleared their orbits. Pluto is the famous one. Ceres is in the asteroid belt. Eris and Makemake are out past Neptune. The stats for each one are on its own page.

Step 2: choose one

  • Pluto

    Used to be the ninth planet. Now it is the best-known dwarf planet.

  • Ceres

    The dwarf planet in the asteroid belt.

  • Eris

    About Pluto's size, but much farther from the Sun.

  • Makemake

    A Kuiper Belt dwarf planet with one known moon.
