Titan is Saturn's largest moon. It is unusual because it has a thick atmosphere. The lakes on Titan are liquid methane and ethane, not water.

Data for Titan

  • Diameter 5,150 km
  • Distance from Saturn 1.2 million km
  • Orbital period 16 Earth days
  • Temperature About -179°C
