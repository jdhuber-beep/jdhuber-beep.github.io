Venus is close to Earth in size, but the surface is nothing like ours. A thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat, which is why Venus is hotter than Mercury even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.

Data for Venus

  • Diameter 12,104 km
  • Distance from the Sun 108.2 million km
  • Orbital period 225 Earth days
  • Temperature About 464°C at the surface
