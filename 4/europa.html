Europa orbits Jupiter. The outside is ice, and there is strong evidence for a saltwater ocean under that ice. That is why it shows up in so many talks about life beyond Earth.

Data for Europa

  • Diameter 3,122 km
  • Distance from Jupiter 671,000 km
  • Orbital period 3.5 Earth days
  • Temperature About -160°C at the surface
