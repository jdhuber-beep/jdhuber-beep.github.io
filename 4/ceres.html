Ceres is the only dwarf planet in the asteroid belt, between Mars and Jupiter. It is much smaller than Pluto. Bright spots on the surface are salt deposits.

Data for Ceres

  • Diameter 940 km
  • Distance from the Sun 414 million km
  • Orbital period 4.6 Earth years
  • Temperature About -105°C
