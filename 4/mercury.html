Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. It does not have a real atmosphere to hold heat, so the ground goes from extremely hot in the day to extremely cold at night.

Data for Mercury

  • Diameter 4,879 km
  • Distance from the Sun 57.9 million km
  • Orbital period 88 Earth days
  • Temperature About 167°C on average, with a huge day-night swing
