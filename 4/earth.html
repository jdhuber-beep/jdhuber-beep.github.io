Earth is the planet we live on. Liquid water stays on the surface, and it is the only place we know that has life. The distance from the Sun is a big part of why the average temperature is mild.

Data for Earth

  • Distance from the Sun 149.6 million km
  • Orbital period 365.25 Earth days
  • Temperature About 15°C on average
