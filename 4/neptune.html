Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant, darker blue than Uranus, and it has the fastest winds measured on any planet.

Data for Neptune

  • Diameter 49,244 km
  • Distance from the Sun 4.5 billion km
  • Orbital period 165 Earth years
  • Temperature About -200°C
