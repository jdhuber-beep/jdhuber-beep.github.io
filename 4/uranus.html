Uranus is an ice giant, not a rocky planet. It rotates on its side, so its seasons are strange compared with Earth. Methane in the air gives it a blue-green color.

Data for Uranus

  • Diameter 50,724 km
  • Distance from the Sun 2.9 billion km
  • Orbital period 84 Earth years
  • Temperature About -195°C
